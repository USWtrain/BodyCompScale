🛠️ Module 02

Know Your Unit & Setup

The Omron Body Fat Scale has a scale with foot electrodes and a display unit with grip electrodes on a retractable cord.

⛔
DO NOT USE with a cardiac pacemaker or any other implanted medical device.

Before you begin

  • DO NOT use on a cushioned floor (carpet or mat). Use a hard, flat surface.
  • Keep cell phones, microwaves, and other electrical devices away.
  • Bare feet; palms and soles clean and dry.
  • Maximum weight: 330 lb.

Know your unit

Display unit: grip electrodes, display and buttons

Set up

  1. Insert 4 AA batteries. The battery cover is on the back of the unit.
  2. First use only: set the time and date.
  3. Press the power switch. CAL flashes, then shows 0.0 lb. Do not step on until it reads 0.0.
  4. Lift the display unit out of its holder.
Power switch: front edge of the scale

Station setup

  1. Place the scale on a hard, flat floor, away from cell phones and other electrical devices.
  2. Set up privacy screens around the scale.
  3. Put a chair nearby so participants can sit to remove their shoes and socks.
  4. Set out consent forms, participant results forms, pens, paper towels, and disinfecting wipes.
⚖️ Module 03

Taking a Measurement

Follow these steps for every participant.

Check in

  1. Greet the participant and explain the process.
  2. Collect a signed consent form.
  3. Ask privately: "Do you have a pacemaker or any other implanted medical device?" and "Are you pregnant, or could you be pregnant?" If the answer to either is yes or unsure, do not measure.
  4. Have them remove their shoes and socks. Palms and soles should be clean and dry.

Enter profile

  1. Press the UP/GUEST button.
  2. Use UP / DOWN to set age, then press SET.
  3. Set gender assigned at birth, then press SET.
  4. Set height, then press SET.

Measure

  1. Participant steps on with bare feet and holds the grip electrodes (see Module 04). They stay still: weight blinks twice, then body composition calculates.
  2. When "start" appears, they extend their arms straight out at 90° and hold until the sequence finishes.

Finish

  1. Participant steps off. Record results on the tablet and the participant results form.
  2. Clear the analyzer before greeting the next participant.
  3. Wipe down the foot electrodes and grip electrodes.
🔒
Privacy: never say results aloud

If the participant asks, point to their result on the results form.

Conversation starters

No script needed — pick whatever fits the moment.

  • "Have you ever had your body fat or muscle measured?"

Putting them at ease

  • "This is just for you — results stay private."
  • "There's no good or bad number here, just information you can use."

After the measurement

  • "Is there anything on your results form you'd like me to explain?"
  • "What's one healthy habit you already have going for you?"
  • "Is there one small change you'd like to try?"
🙆 Module 04

Grip & Posture

Incorrect posture may result in an inaccurate measurement of body composition.

Correct grip

  1. Place both middle fingers along the dents of the grip electrodes.
  2. Hold the inner grip electrodes firmly with the thumb and index finger.
  3. Hold the outer grip electrodes with the ring finger and little finger.
  4. Press the palms firmly on the grip electrodes.
Correct grip, left to right

Correct posture

Correct posture: arms straight out at 90°
Postures to avoid
  • Stand up straight with both feet centered on the foot electrodes — not on the edge of the scale.
  • Keep knees straight and stay still until the sequence finishes.
  • Extend arms straight out at 90° — not bent, and not too low or too high.
  • Keep the display facing forward, not facing up.
🔧 Module 05

Troubleshooting

If an error code appears, check the fix below and take the measurement again.

Err 1Palms or feet not on electrodes. Check bare feet, clean dry skin, posture, and grip.
Err 2Position not stable. Re-place palms and feet correctly.
Err 3Skin too dry. Lightly moisten palms or soles with a damp paper towel.
Err 4Body composition values are outside the measurement range.
Err 5Monitor not set up correctly. Remove batteries, wait 1 minute, reinstall, and retry.
ErrStepped on or moved before 0.0 lb, movement during reading, or weight over 330 lb.

📊 Module 06

Interpreting Results

Use these tables to understand each participant's ratings. Results are recorded on the tablet and the participant results form — never said aloud.

How the scale works

The scale uses bioelectrical impedance: it sends a very weak electrical current (50 kHz, under 500 microamps) through the body. Muscle, blood, and other water-rich tissue conduct electricity easily; fat holds little water and conducts poorly. Omron's algorithm combines this reading with height, weight, age, and gender.

Visceral fat surrounds the organs and is linked to high blood fat levels, high cholesterol, cardiovascular disease, and diabetes. Subcutaneous fat sits below the skin; it is not directly linked to increased disease risk but is thought to add pressure on the heart.

📦 Module 07

Breakdown & Cleanup

End-of-event teardown — keep every form private until it's packed.

  1. Gather all signed consent forms and return all paperwork to US Wellness.
  2. Wipe down the scale and grip electrodes, turn the scale off, return the display unit to its holder, and pack it carefully.
  3. Pack up privacy screens and remaining supplies, and handle them as described in your event notes.
  4. Leave the space clean and put any furniture back where you found it.
